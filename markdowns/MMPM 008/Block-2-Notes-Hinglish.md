## Unit 4: Rural Buying Behaviour and Influencing Factors

### 1. Consumer Buying Decision Process
Urban ke individualistic buying patterns ke mukable rural India mein buying process bohot **collective aur community-validated** (samuhik aur aapsi sehmati) hota hai:

1. **Problem Recognition:** Yeh luxury ke bajaye utility aur functional needs par based hota hai. Need trigger karne ke liye marketers live demos ka use karte hain (e.g. Asian Paints ne post office ki deewar par *Utsav* distemper paint ka demo dikhaya taaki log paint peeling problem ka solution dekh kar trust kar sakein).
2. **Information Search:** Commercial ads ke bajaye log **personal sources** (family, Sarpanch, village pradhan) aur **experiential sources** (product touch-and-feel, trial packs, haats ke chakkar) par zyada rely karte hain.
3. **Evaluation of Alternatives:** Rural consumers price, quality, aur chalane ki simplicity (ease of use) ko dekhte hain. High-involvement durables (tractors, TVs) ke liye evaluation cycle lambi hoti hai; daily products ke liye choti.
4. **Purchase Decision:** Perceived risk, dusro ke attitude, aur cash availability se decision badal sakta hai. Final decision aksar pure family ya opinion leaders ki advice par liya jata hai.
5. **Post-purchase Behaviour:** Accha experience strong brand loyalty banata hai. Agar product kharab nikle toh negative word-of-mouth (WOM) close-knit village networks mein bohot tezi se failta hai.

---

### 2. Social Class aur SEC Grid
Multi-occupations aur seasonal agriculture cycles ki wajah se rural areas mein social class define karna mushkil hai. Marketers MRSI dwara banaye gaye **Socio-Economic Classification (SEC)** grid ka use karte hain:

* **Yeh do criteria par based hai:**
  1. Chief wage earner (ghar ke main kamane wale) ka education level.
  2. House type (Pucca, Semi-Pucca, ya Kachha).
* **Classification Scale: R1 se R4**
  * **R1 & R2:** Higher consuming classes (e.g., pucca ghar mein rehne wala graduate/higher secondary pass earner). Yeh high-involvement durables kharidte hain aur bulk shopping ke liye sheher jate hain.
  * **R3 & R4:** Lower consuming, poor families. Yeh local village kirana aur weekly haats par depend hote hain.

---

### 3. Reference Groups aur Opinion Leaders ka Role
Reference groups logo ke attitude ko shape karte hain, naye products introduce karte hain, aur purchase decisions ko validate karte hain:

* **Opinion Leaders:** Village Sarpanch, Panchayat members, school teachers, ya sheher se laute migrant workers. Smartphone aur bikes jaise high-tech products ke liye ghar ke educated youth naye opinion leaders ban rahe hain.
* **Marketer Applied Examples:**
  * **Hero Honda:** *"Har Gaaon Har Aangan"* campaign ke under local village youth ko sales executives recruit kiya. Yeh log direct selling nahi balki aam baatchit ke zariye logo ka trust jeette the.
  * **Tata Shakti:** Local *Mistris* (masons) ko opinion leaders banaya jo roof sheets recommend karte the, jisse customers *"63 no. wala chaddar"* demand karne lage.
  * **HUL:** Gaon ki women ko *Shakti Ammas* banakar household products bechne ke liye trusted local opinion leaders banaya.
  * **Colgate:** *"Bright Smiles, Bright Futures"* campaign mein school teachers ko influencers banaya.

## Unit 5: Trends in Consumer Behaviour

### 1. Financial Inclusion & Community Empowerment
* **Financial Inclusion:** Jan Dhan Accounts (PMJDY) aur accident insurance (PMSBY) se financial awareness badi hai aur DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) se logo ke haath mein seedha paisa pahunch raha hai, jisse unki buying power badi hai.
* **Community Empowerment:** Panchayats mein women ke liye 50% reservation aur **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)** ki success se women financial-independent hui hain aur family purchasing mein unki voice strong hui hai.

### 2. Trends in Rural Consumption Patterns
* **"Individualized Joint Families":** Ghar ek hi hota hai par kitchens aur spending alag hote hain taaki aapsi ladai na ho. Isse badal kar log ab single bulk pack ke bajaye ek hi product ke multiple small units kharidne lage hain.
* **Aspirational Spending:** Miscellaneous categories jaise bacho ki education, family health, two-wheelers, aur digital services par kharch badh raha hai.
* **Brand Shift:** TV ads aur mobile usage ki wajah se brands (Royal Enfield, LG, Samsung) ki demand badh rahi hai.

## Unit 6: Rural Marketing Research

### 1. Qualitative Approach vs. Survey Method
Low literacy levels aur language barrier ki wajah se rigid quantitative survey questionnaires gaon mein fail ho jate hain. **Qualitative aur observational research** yahan best hai kyunki yeh non-verbal cues ko capture karti hai, local customs ka respect karti hai, aur villagers ke sath trust banati hai.

---

### 2. Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA)
Aisa collaborative social research method jismein villagers data collect karne mein khud actively participate karte hain:

* **Transect Walk:** Researcher gaon mein ghumkar daily habits, infrastructure, aur facilities ko observe karta hai.
* **Village Mapping:** Villagers rangoli powder ya koyle se zameen par ya board par gaon ke resources ka map banate hain.
* **Time Line:** Villagers purane time se lekar ab tak ki main events ki list banate hain.
* **Seasonality Diagram:** Alag-alag seasons ke according data (crop prices, illness, water scarcity) map karte hain.
* **Matrix Ranking:** Pathar, beej, ya symbols use karke log brands ya preferences ko rank karte hain.
* **Venn Diagrams (Chapattis):** Alag-alag size ke circles banakar different organizations aur unke importance ko rank karte hain.

---

### 3. Key Qualitative Research Techniques
* **Participant Observation:** Researcher gaon ke logon ke sath rehkar unke rituals mein participate karta hai taaki customs ki real meaning samajh sake.
* **Projective Visual Tools:** Clean cartoon drawings ya **Thematic Apperception Tests (TAT)** ka use karna, kyunki complex photos ke mukable simple caricatures ko rural consumer jaldi samajh jata hai.
* **Scale Simplification:** 5 ya 7-point Likert scales ke bajaye simple **3-point pictorial scales** (e.g. smileys - happy/neutral/sad) use karna.

## Unit 7: Mobile Phone Industry Case Study

* **Growth Drivers:** Handsets ke prices kam hone aur cheap data plans ki wajah se mobile ownership bohot tezi se bada.
* **Behavioral Shifts:** Mobile sirf baat karne ka tool nahi raha, balki banking, weather updates, crop price check karne, aur entertainment ka main source ban gaya.
* **Purchase Influences:** Rural youth aur sheher se laute migrant workers handset brands choose karne mein main influencers ka kaam karte hain.
