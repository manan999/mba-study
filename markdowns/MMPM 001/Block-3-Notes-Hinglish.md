### UNIT 9: REFERENCE GROUP INFLUENCE AND GROUP DYNAMICS

#### 1. Concept of Reference Groups and Types
*   **Definition:** Koi bhi person ya group jo kisi individual ke values, attitudes ya behavior ko guide karne ke liye comparison point (reference) ka kaam karta hai.
*   **Types of Reference Groups:**
    1.  *Membership vs. Non-Membership:* Jis group ke hum member hote hain vs. jiske hum member nahi hote par uska influence hum par hota hai.
    2.  *Formal vs. Informal:* Jisme rules aur structured list hoti hai (jaise professional association) vs. loose group jo dosti par based ho (jaise peer group, neighbors).
    3.  *Primary vs. Secondary:* Jisme roz face-to-face aur close interaction ho (jaise family, close friends) vs. jisme regular aur personal talk na ho (jaise business club, shopping group).
    4.  *Aspirational Groups:* Jis group ka part consumer banna chahta hai.
        *   *Anticipatory:* Jahan aane wale time mein join karne ki umeed ho (jaise junior management ka senior club join karne ka sochna).
        *   *Symbolic:* Jise hum admire karte hain par membership milna mushkil hai (jaise sports teams). Marketers iske liye celebrities ko branding mein use karte hain.
    5.  *Dissociative (Negative) Groups:* Jis group ke sath consumer bilkul associate nahi hona chahta (jaise specific clothing style ko avoid karna jo unhe pasand na ho).

#### 2. Types of Reference Group Influences
*   **Normative (Utilitarian) Influence:** Group ke norms aur behaviors ko follow karna taaki social reward mile ya embarrassment se bacha ja sake (jaise corporate party mein formal dress peenna).
*   **Comparative (Value-Expressive) Influence:** Dusron se khud ko compare karke apni identity express karna (jaise doston ke group mein smoking karna kyunki wahan use "cool" mana jata hai).
*   **Informational Influence:** Kisi credible aur expert source se advice lena (jaise tech-savvy dost se naya phone purchase karne ke liye puchna).

#### 3. Conspicuousness Matrix: Influence on Product vs. Brand Choice
Reference group ka influence kisi product ke *Conspicuousness* (exclusivity aur visibility) par depend karta hai:

| Dimension | Public (Visible Consumption) | Private (Hidden Consumption) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Luxury (Exclusive)** | **Public Luxuries** (e.g., Premium Smartphone, Fashion Clothes)<br>• *Product Influence:* Strong<br>• *Brand Influence:* Strong | **Private Luxuries** (e.g., Home Theater, Espresso Maker)<br>• *Product Influence:* Strong<br>• *Brand Influence:* Weak |
| **Necessity (Common)** | **Public Necessities** (e.g., Wristwatch, Automobile)<br>• *Product/Brand Influence:* Brand: Strong, Product: Weak | **Private Necessities** (e.g., Floor Cleaner, Water Filter)<br>• *Product/Brand Influence:* Product: Weak, Brand: Weak |

*   *Examples:*
    *   *Fashion Clothing:* Public necessity hai. Visibility high hai par necessity common hai. Isme reference group *brand* par strong influence daalta hai (Zara vs. H&M) par product class par nahi.
    *   *Mobile Phone:* Public luxury/necessity hai. Visibility high hai aur brand status se logo ke opinions affect hote hain. Reference group *product aur brand* dono par strong influence rakhta hai (jaise iPhone purchase karna).
    *   *Life Insurance:* Private necessity hai. Low visibility aur high complexity. Peer pressure nahi hota, log security needs ke hisab se decision lete hain.
    *   *Charcoal Water Filter:* Private necessity hai. Kitchen ke andar rehta hai aur public ko visible nahi hota. Isliye reference group ka impact zero hota hai, purchase purely price aur performance par hoti hai.

#### 4. Bases of Social Power
1.  *Reward Power:* Social reward ya acceptance dene ki power (jaise trendy clothes peene par group validation milna).
2.  *Coercive Power:* Negative reaction dene ki power (jaise ads mein body odor ya bad breath dikhakar sharmindagi se bachne ke liye deos aur mouthwash promote karna).
3.  *Legitimate Power:* Cultural ya official rules se aane wali power (jaise dress codes).
4.  *Referent Power:* Aspiration ya admiration se aane wali power (jaise consumers celebrity endorsements ko copy karte hain).
5.  *Expert Power:* Knowledge aur expertise se aane wali power (jaise dentist dwara toothpaste suggest kiya jana).

#### 5. Opinion Leadership
*   **Definition:** Woh process jisme ek person (opinion leader) informally dusre logon (opinion seekers) ke product attitudes aur actions ko influence karta hai.
*   **Characteristics:** Yeh product-specific hote hain, inka us category mein involvement high hota hai, self-confident hote hain, aur socially active hote hain.
*   **Transmission Models:**
    *   *Two-Step Flow:* Media $\to$ Opinion Leaders $\to$ General Public.
    *   *Multi-Step Flow:* Media, Opinion Leaders, Gatekeepers, aur Receivers ke beech ka interactive flow.

#### 6. Social Class Concept and Marketing Applications
*   **Definition:** Society ke relatively permanent aur homogeneous divisions jisme logon ke lifestyle, interests, aur behaviors similar hote hain.
*   **Indicators (Warner's ISC):** Chaar main variables se check hota hai:
    1. *Occupation* (sabse important) | 2. *Source of Income* (inherited vs. salary) | 3. *House Type* | 4. *Dwelling Area*
*   **Consumer Choices by Social Class:**
    *   *Housing:* Upper class safety aur prestige (exclusive villas) ko value deta hai. Middle class functional flats prefer karta hai. Lower class affordability dekhta hai.
    *   *Holidays:* Upper class exotic aur customized international tours chahta hai. Middle class domestic family tours choose karta hai. Working class local/religious tours par jata hai.
    *   *Banking Services:* Upper class ko private wealth management chahiye. Middle class salary accounts aur mutual funds (SIPs) use karta hai. Lower class zero-balance accounts aur micro-loans leta hai.

### UNIT 10: FAMILY BUYING INFLUENCES, FAMILY LIFE CYCLE AND BUYING ROLES

#### 1. The Family as a Social Group
*   **Kyun hai ek Social Group?** Family ek primary group hai jahan close, face-to-face interaction, emotional bonding, aur values sharing hoti hai. Yeh consumption aur socialization ki sabse basic unit hai.

#### 2. Traditional Family Life Cycle (FLC) Stages
1.  *Bachelor Stage:* Young aur single. Low income par koi financial burden nahi. Lifestyle, gadgets, aur travel par spend karte hain.
2.  *Newly Married:* Nayi shadi, koi bacche nahi. Consumer durables, home appliances, aur furniture par high expense.
3.  *Full Nest 1:* Young married, baccha under 6 years. Income thodi kam ho sakti hai agar ek partner job chhode. Baby food, toys, aur medical bills par high spend.
4.  *Full Nest 2:* Older married, baccha over 6 years. Financial position better hoti hai. Education, bicycles, aur family health par focus.
5.  *Full Nest 3:* Older married, dependent young kids. Experience aur value-conscious buyers. High education fees aur replacement buying.
6.  *Empty Nest:* Children completed education aur ghar chhod chuke hain. Peak savings aur maximum stability. Travel, medical care, aur self-improvement gadgets par spend.
7.  *Solitary Survivor:* Older, single, retired. Income drastic drop hoti hai. Main focus medicine aur healthcare services par hota hai.
*   *Note:* *Modern FLC* mein single parents, childless couples (DINKs) shamil hote hain jo convenience/time-saving goods zyada buy karte hain.

#### 3. Joint Decision-Making in Families
Chaar main types mein decisions divide hote hain:
1.  *Husband-Dominant:* Final decision husband leta hai (e.g., life insurance, car, financial investments).
2.  *Wife-Dominant:* Final decision wife leti hai (e.g., grocery, kitchen utensils, kids' daily wear).
3.  *Joint / Syncratic:* Dono milkar decide karte hain (e.g., flat buy karna, bachon ke liye school, family tour).
4.  *Autonomic:* Koi bhi ek partner independently buy kar leta hai (e.g., personal care cosmetics, office wear).

#### 4. Consumer Socialization of Children
*   **Definition:** Woh process jiske through bacche markets mein ek consumer ki tarah perform karne ke liye zaroori skills, knowledge, aur attitudes seekhte hain.
*   **Socializing Agents:** Parents (rational budget buying seekhate hain), Peers (social/brand conformity seekhate hain), aur TV/Digital Media (emotional/hedonic desires stimulate karte hain).
*   **Learning Processes:**
    *   *Imitation/Modeling:* Parents ke brands ko copy karna (jisse generational brand loyalty banti hai).
    *   *Operant Conditioning:* Finshing healthy meal par praise milna vs. waste karne par gussa hona.
    *   *Financial Autonomy:* Pocket money aur allowances se paise handle karna seekhna.

### UNIT 11: CULTURE AND SUBCULTURAL INFLUENCES

#### 1. Concept and Characteristics of Culture
*   **Definition:** Values, beliefs, customs aur ideas ka ek complex set jo human behavior ko guide karta hai aur generation to generation transfer hota hai.
*   **Components:**
    *   *Internal Mental Culture:* Cognitive (gods/afterlife beliefs) aur Normative (rules, values, moral codes).
    *   *External Material Culture:* Tangible objects jaise art, technology, clothes, aur housing.
*   **Characteristics:** Culture *invented* hota hai, *learned* hota hai (imitation aur instruction se), *shared* hota hai, *gratifying & persistent*, *dynamic & adaptive*, *integrated*, aur *prescriptive* hota hai.

#### 2. Indian Sub-cultures and Consumption Patterns
Heterogeneous national culture ke andar homogeneous subgroups ko sub-cultures kehte hain:
*   **Religious Sub-cultures:** (e.g., Hindus, Muslims, Sikhs, Jains, Christians).
    *   *Consumption Mores:* Festivals (Diwali, Eid, Christmas) par kapde, sweets, aur gifts ki heavy buying hoti hai.
    *   *Taboos:* Muslims ke liye halal and non-alcohol, Jains ke liye strict vegetarianism/no root vegetables, aur Hindus ke liye beef restriction.
*   **Geographical/Regional Sub-cultures:** (e.g., Bengali, Punjabi, Tamil, Gujarati).
    *   *Food Habits:* South India mein rice, coconut oil, aur filter coffee prefer hoti hai. North India mein wheat, ghee/butter, aur tea. Bengal mein mustard oil aur fish.
    *   *Clothing:* South mein silk saris (Kanchipuram) popular hain, North-West mein cottons, aur North mein winter seasons mein woolens.
    *   *Language:* 22 official regional languages ki wajah se localized marketing communication zaroori hai.

#### 3. Cross-Cultural Consumer Behaviour
*   **Standardization (Global Approach):** Poore world mein ek hi product aur campaign bechna (jaise Apple iPhone ya Rolex). Consumer needs ko homogeneous mana jata hai.
*   **Localization (Adaptive Approach):** Product aur marketing mix ko local cultural values ke hisab se change karna (jaise McDonald's ka India mein *McAloo Tikki* ya *Veg Maharaja Mac* bechna, aur Kellogg's ka milk habits ke according product adapt karna).
