### UNIT 1: CONSUMER BEHAVIOUR — NATURE, SCOPE, MODELS AND APPLICATIONS

#### 1. Nature and Scope of Consumer Behaviour
*   **Definition:** Consumer behaviour basically woh decision process aur physical activity hai jab koi consumer goods aur services ko evaluate (check), acquire (buy), use (upayog), ya dispose (discard) karta hai. Isme mental aur emotional responses bhi shamil hain jo purchase se pehle, uske dauran, aur uske baad hote hain.
*   **Scope:** Loudon aur Della Bitta ke framework ke according, iske scope ko teen main sections mein divide kiya ja sakta hai:
    1.  *The Decision Process:* Problem recognition (need pehchanna), information search, alternative evaluation (options compare karna), purchase, aur post-purchase evaluation.
    2.  *Individual Determinants:* Motivation aur involvement, attitudes, personality aur self-concept, learning aur memory, aur information processing.
    3.  *External Environment / Group Influences:* Culture, sub-culture, social class, social groups, family, aur personal influences.
*   **Key Roles in Buying Situations:**
    *   *Initiator:* Jo sabse pehle pehchanta hai ki kisi product ya service ki need hai.
    *   *Influencer:* Jo intentionally ya unintentionally purchase decision ko guide ya affect karta hai.
    *   *Buyer:* Jo actual physical transaction (payment/purchase) karta hai.
    *   *User:* Jo end mein product/service ko consume ya use karta hai.

#### 2. Models of Consumer Behaviour
*   **Howard-Sheth Model:** Yeh model decision-making ke teen levels ko explain karta hai:
    1.  *Extensive Problem Solving:* Jab buyer ko brand ya product ke baare mein koi basic info nahi hoti aur woh bohot detailed information dhoodhta hai.
    2.  *Limited Problem Solving:* Jab buyer ke paas thodi-bahut info hoti hai aur woh options ko compare karne ke liye information dhoodhta hai.
    3.  *Routinised Response Behaviour:* Jab buyer ke paas poori info hoti hai, woh brands ko achhe se janta hai, aur fataphat purchase kar leta hai.
    4.  *Variables:* Inputs (price, quality, social status), Perceptual and Learning Constructs (motives, attitudes, perceptual bias), Outputs (purchase decision, satisfaction), aur Exogenous Variables (purchase ki importance, time pressure).
*   **Engel-Blackwell-Miniard (EBM) Model:** Ek comprehensive cognitive processing model jisme five core components hote hain:
    1.  *Information Input:* Marketer stimuli (ads, displays) aur non-market stimuli (family opinions, reviews).
    2.  *Information Processing:* Exposure, attention, comprehension, acceptance, aur retention (memory ka use karke).
    3.  *Decision Process:* Problem recognition $\to$ Search for alternatives $\to$ Alternative evaluation $\to$ Choice $\to$ Post-purchase outcomes.
    4.  *Decision Process Variables:* Demographics, motives, beliefs, attitude, values, aur lifestyle.
    5.  *External Influences:* Culture, social class, family, aur reference groups.

#### 3. Online Consumer Behaviour & Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)
*   **Online Buying Process vs. Physical Store Buying:**
    1.  *Information Access:* Online shopping mein 24/7 access milta hai reviews aur price comparison ka, jabki physical shopping mein dukano par jana padta hai.
    2.  *Convenience:* Online shopping physical effort aur time bachati hai, jabki physical stores product ko touch aur feel karne ka advantage dete hain.
    3.  *Payment & Delivery:* Online mein digital wallets/cards ya COD ka use hota hai aur delivery lag hota hai. Physical store mein immediate possession milta hai.
*   **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) by Fred Davis:** Yeh batata hai ki koi consumer online buy karega ya nahi, yeh do mental factors par depend karta hai:
    1.  *Perceived Usefulness (PU):* Consumer ko kitna lagta hai ki online shopping se uska time bachega aur transaction easily ho jayega.
    2.  *Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU):* Online shopping platform use karne mein kitna simple, user-friendly aur convenient hai.
    *   *Outcome:* PU aur PEOU se consumer ka *Attitude* aur *Behavioral Intention* banta hai, jo end mein *Actual Use* mein badal jata hai.
    *   *Example:* Ek busy professional ko naya geyser chahiye. Dukano par jane ke bajay woh phone par e-commerce app open karta hai (PEOU), specifications aur user reviews dekhta hai, discounts aur next-day installation check karta hai (PU), aur 15 minutes mein payment karke order complete kar deta hai.

#### 4. Marketing Applications of Consumer Behaviour
1.  **Analyzing Market Opportunities:** Customers ki unfulfilled needs ko analyze karna (jaise working women ke badhne se time-saving appliances ki demand badh rahi hai).
2.  **Target Market Selection:** Buyer traits ke basis par segment karna (jaise rural markets ke liye saste shampoo sachets launch karna).
3.  **Product Mix Decisions:** Product design aur packaging decide karna (jaise Maggi ka regional flavors lana ya zip-lock pouches banana).
4.  **Pricing Decisions:** Price sensitivity aur discounts ke perception ko samajhna.
5.  **Distribution Strategy:** Channels select karna (jaise complex products like vacuum cleaners ke liye direct-to-home personal selling use karna taaki demo diya ja sake).
6.  **Promotion Strategy:** Target segment ke preferences ke hisab se media (digital/print/TV) select karna.
7.  **Non-Profit & Social Marketing:** Public welfare campaigns ko effectively design karna (jaise tax payment ke liye friendly awareness ads chalana).

### UNIT 2: CONSUMER BEHAVIOUR AND LIFESTYLE MARKETING

#### 1. Concept of Lifestyle and Psychographics
*   **Lifestyle:** Kisi person ke jeene ka tarika, jo uski Activities, Interests aur Opinions (AIO) mein dikhta hai. Yeh batata hai ki log apna time aur paisa kaise spend karte hain.
*   **Psychographics:** Lifestyles ko quantitatively measure karne aur consumer profiles banane ki technique.
*   **AIO Inventories:** Iska use psychographics measure karne ke liye hota hai:
    *   *Activities:* Work, hobbies, social events, shopping.
    *   *Interests:* Family, home, job, recreation, fashion.
    *   *Opinions:* Themselves, social issues, politics, products.

#### 2. The VALS (Values and Lifestyles) Classification
SRI International dwara banaya gaya VALS system consumers ko psychological traits (self-orientation) aur resources ke basis par divide karta hai.
*   **Three Self-Orientations:**
    1.  *Principles-Oriented:* Jo apne internal beliefs aur values ke hisab se decision lete hain.
    2.  *Status-Oriented:* Jo dusron ke opinions aur status se heavily influence hote hain.
    3.  *Action-Oriented:* Jo activity, variety aur adventure pasand karte hain.
*   **Resources:** High se Low ki range mein (income, education, confidence, health).
*   **Eight VALS-2 Lifestyle Segments:**
    1.  *Innovators:* Sabse high resources wale log. Sophisticated aur active hote hain.
    2.  *Thinkers:* Principles-oriented aur high resources wale. Reflective, mature aur durability ko value dene wale.
    3.  *Believers:* Principles-oriented aur modest resources wale. Conservative aur traditional brands ko pasand karte hain.
    4.  *Achievers:* Status-oriented aur high resources wale. Work-oriented, risk-averse aur status symbols pasand karne wale.
    5.  *Strivers:* Status-oriented aur low resources wale. Approval-seeking aur high-resource groups ko copy karne wale.
    6.  *Experiencers:* Action-oriented aur high resources wale. Young, impulsive, fashion/music par spend karne wale.
    7.  *Makers:* Action-oriented aur modest resources wale. Practical, hands-on kaam karne wale (unimpressed by status).
    8.  *Survivors:* Sabse low resources wale. Safety aur survival needs par focused, specific brands ke liye loyal.

#### 3. Applications of Lifestyle Marketing
*   **Positioning New Products:** Kisi specific lifestyle niche ko target karna (jaise design-conscious *Innovators* ke liye premium home-decor launch karna).
*   **Repositioning Existing Products:** Brand image ko badalte lifestyles ke according change karna (jaise normal tea ko green/herbal health tea ki tarah reposition karna).
*   **Media Selection:** Ads ko right platform par dikhana (jaise adventure sports gear ke ads digital channels par dikhana jo *Experiencers* dekhte hain).
*   **Indian Context & Social Influences:** Online shopping patterns status aur life cycle ke sath change hote hain (jaise young couples online electronics/clothes zyada buy karte hain, jabki older nests health/family insurance ko priority dete hain).

### UNIT 3: ORGANISATIONAL BUYING BEHAVIOUR

#### 1. Consumer vs. Organizational Buying Behaviour
*   **Market Structure:** Organizational markets mein buyers kam hote hain lekin unka size bada hota hai. Demand *derived* hoti hai (consumer demand par dependent).
*   **Buying Center Complexity:** Consumer buying mein family members involve hote hain, jabki organizational buying mein ek formal, multi-person cross-functional group hota hai jise **Buying Center** kehte hain.
*   **Decision Length & Process:** B2B decisions bohot formal hote hain, administrative procedures follow karte hain aur inme legal contracts hote hain.

#### 2. The Buying Center Roles
*   *Users:* Jo product ko use karenge (jaise factory workers).
*   *Influencers:* Jo technical specifications set karte hain (jaise consultants/engineers).
*   *Deciders:* Jinke paas final choice ka power hota hai (jaise management/committee).
*   *Buyers:* Jinke paas vendor select karne aur commercial terms manage karne ki formal authority hoti hai.
*   *Gatekeepers:* Jo information ke flow ko control karte hain (jaise secretary ya assistant).
*   *Specifiers:* Jo product ke technical requirements aur specs ko draft karte hain.

#### 3. The Buy Grid Model
Robinson, Faris aur Wind ke according, yeh model **3 Buy Classes** aur **8 Buy Phases** ka matrix hai:

| Buy Phase / Stage | Straight Re-buy (Routine) | Modified Re-buy (Upgrade) | New Task (Naya Product) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Problem Recognition** | Automatic / Routine | Moderate Review | Detailed Analysis |
| **2. Determination of Characteristics** | Pre-defined | Some Changes | Thorough Research |
| **3. Description of Specifications** | Pre-established | Minor Edits | Custom Drafted |
| **4. Supplier Search** | Skipped | Limited Search | Extensive Search |
| **5. Acquisition of Proposals** | None | Few Quotes | Detailed RFPs (Tenders) |
| **6. Evaluation & Selection** | Automated | Competitive Bidding | Multi-stage Committee |
| **7. Order-Routine Selection** | Automated | Semi-automated | Custom Terms |
| **8. Performance Feedback** | Routine Check | Occasional Review | Formal Evaluation |

*   *Straight Re-buy:* Low risk, regular items reorder karna (jaise office stationary).
*   *Modified Re-buy:* Moderate risk, specs ya supplier change karna (jaise office ke laptops upgrade karna).
*   *New Task:* High risk, pehli baar koi cheez buy karna (jaise ERP software implement karna).

#### 4. Fisher's Model of Organizational Buying Behaviour
Product complexity aur Commercial uncertainty ke basis par decisions divide hote hain:
*   *Cell 1 (Low Complexity, Low Uncertainty):* Purchase department dominates. Routine decisions.
*   *Cell 2 (High Complexity, Low Uncertainty):* Technical experts and engineers dominate. Specs are key.
*   *Cell 3 (Low Complexity, High Uncertainty):* Finance and top management dominate. Cost and commercial terms are key.
*   *Cell 4 (High Complexity, High Uncertainty):* Joint decision-making by a multi-disciplinary committee.

#### 5. Major Influences on Organizational Buying
*   *Environmental Factors:* Economic conditions, political landscape (jaise *Atmanirbhar Bharat* policy), legal rules, technology change.
*   *Organisational Factors:* Company objectives, structure, resources, and rules.
*   *Interpersonal Factors:* Power dynamics, authority, conflict resolution (rational problem-solving/persuasion or bargaining/politicking).
*   *Individual Factors:* Demographics, personality, and risk tolerance of decision-makers.

#### 6. Illustrative Application: Purchase of CCTV Cameras
*   **Individual Purchase (Consumer):** Ek ghar ke liye CCTV chahiye. Decision fataphat ho jata hai, family members decide kar lete hain, online review dekh kar credit card se buy kar lete hain. Koi formal specification sheet ya contract nahi hota.
*   **Organizational Purchase (B2B):** Ek bank ko apni 500 branches ke liye CCTV lagwane hain. Yahan Buying Center bada hoga (IT head specifications banayega, Security officer verify karega, Finance manager rate negotiate karega, Purchase manager deal execute karega). Tenders nikale jayenge, site testing hogi, warranties aur annual maintenance contracts (AMC) sign honge. Yeh Fisher's Model ke *Cell 4* (High Complexity, High Uncertainty) mein aayega.
