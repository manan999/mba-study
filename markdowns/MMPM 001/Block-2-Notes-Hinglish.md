### UNIT 4: PERCEPTIONS

#### 1. Concept of Perception vs. Sensation
*   **Sensation:** Hamare sensory receptors (eyes, ears, nose, mouth, skin) ka basic stimuli (light, color, sound, odor, texture) ke prati immediate aur direct response. Yeh raw, unprocessed sensory input hota hai.
*   **Perception:** Woh process jiske dwara log in raw sensations ko select, organize, aur interpret karte hain taaki duniya ki ek meaningful aur clear picture ban sake. Yeh highly subjective hota hai, yaani consumer ki internal reality uski apni interpretation par depend karti hai, na ki objective metrics par.
*   **Stages of Perception:**
    1.  *Primitive Categorization:* Stimulus ke basic features ko group karna (jaise peeli bottle aur nimbu ki photo dekh kar samajhna ki yeh lemon juice hai).
    2.  *Cue Check:* Specific cues ko analyze karna (jaise pack ki shape, colors, aur layout compare karna).
    3.  *Confirmation Check:* In cues ko dimag mein pehle se bani image/schema se match karna (jaise confirm karna ki product "beverage" category ka hai).
    4.  *Confirmation Completion:* Finalize karna aur uske according act karna (jaise drink peena).

#### 2. Sensory Thresholds and Weber's Law
*   **Absolute Threshold:** Kisi stimulus ki intensity ka woh minimum level jo hamare sensory channels ko notice karne ke liye zaroori hai. Agar stimulus isse niche hai, toh woh notice nahi hoga (jaise bohot chhota print billboard par jo drivers ko dikhe hi na).
*   **Differential Threshold:** Do stimuli ke beech ke difference ya change ko detect karne ki capability.
*   **Just Noticeable Difference (JND) & Weber's Law:**
    *   *JND:* Stimulus mein hone wala woh minimum change jise notice kiya ja sake.
    *   *Weber's Law:* Initial stimulus jitna strong hoga, change ko notice karne ke liye us change ka utna hi bada hona zaroori hai.
    *   *Formula:* $\frac{\Delta I}{I} = K$ (jahan $I$ original intensity hai, $\Delta I$ change hai, aur $K$ constant hai).
    *   *Marketing Applications:*
        *   *Same rakhne ke liye:* Logo ya packaging update karte waqt, changes JND se *kam* hone chahiye taaki consumers familiarity na khoyen (jaise Pepsi ya Cadbury ke subtle updates).
        *   *Difference dikhane ke liye:* Discounts, size increase ya quality updates JND se *zyada* hone chahiye taaki customer ka dhyan turant jaye.

#### 3. Subliminal Perception: Does it Work?
*   **Definition:** Aise stimuli ko process karna jo hamare conscious awareness ke absolute threshold se niche (below threshold) hote hain.
*   **Subliminal Techniques:**
    *   *Embeds:* Ads mein chhupi hui tiny images ya shapes.
    *   *Auditory Messages:* Self-help audios mein bohot low volume par ya reverse chalti hui aawazein.
    *   **Does it work?** Nahi, is baat ka koi scientific proof nahi hai ki subliminal ads real life mein consumer buying behaviour ko affect karte hain.
    *   *Kyun fail hota hai?*
        1.  *Individual Thresholds:* Har insaan ka absolute threshold alag hota hai. Jo ek ke liye subliminal hai, dusre ko dikh sakta hai.
        2.  *Attention Control:* Kisi message ko samajhne ke liye attention zaroori hai. Subliminal messages attention gate ko bypass kar dete hain.
        3.  *Action Control:* Subliminal messages itne weak hote hain ki woh consumer ke conscious beliefs aur goals ko override nahi kar sakte.

#### 4. Case Study Applications: Perceptions
*   **Advertising ka Perception par impact:** Ads brand image/schema banate hain. Jaise *Patanjali Dant Kanti* ne herbal cues aur traditional doctors ka use karke toothpaste ke perception ko refreshing/cosmetic se ayurvedic/health-oriented mein badal diya.
*   **Extended Product Offerings (e.g., Raymond + Khadi Commission):** Jab ek premium brand (Raymond) kisi traditional brand (Khadi) ke sath partner karta hai, toh Khadi ka perception rustic/cheap se premium heritage wear mein badal jata hai, aur young style-conscious consumers bhi ise buy karne lagte hain.

### UNIT 5: LEARNING AND MEMORY

#### 1. Concept of Learning
*   **Definition:** Direct ya indirect experience ke kaaran behaviour mein aane wala ek relatively permanent change.
*   **Incidental Learning:** Unintentional ya casual tarike se knowledge milna (jaise achanak se kisi ad ka jingle dimag mein baith jana).

#### 2. Theories of Learning
*   **Classical Conditioning (Pavlov):** Association ke dwara seekhna. Ek neutral stimulus (Conditioned Stimulus - CS) ko Unconditioned Stimulus (UCS) ke sath pair kiya jata hai jo naturally ek response deta hai. Kuch time baad, CS akele hi response dene lagta hai (jaise sound sunte hi mouth-watering hona).
    *   *Stimulus Generalization:* CS se milte-julte stimuli par bhi same response dena (jaise copycat brands ka packaging original brand jaisa banana).
    *   *Stimulus Discrimination:* Do similar stimuli ke beech difference seekhna (jaise original brand ka ad banana ki "saste nakal se bachein").
*   **Operant / Instrumental Conditioning (B.F. Skinner):** Aise behaviours seekhna jo positive reward dein aur negative outcomes (punishment) se bachayein.
    *   *Positive Reinforcement:* Reward behaviour ko strong banata hai (jaise perfume lagane par compliments milna).
    *   *Negative Reinforcement:* Kisi negative situation ko hatana behaviour ko strong banata hai (jaise ad dikhana ki kharab joota tootne par sharmindagi hui, isliye durable brand khareedein).
    *   *Schedules of Reinforcement:*
        *   *Fixed-Interval:* Fixed time ke baad reward (jaise diwali clearance sales).
        *   *Variable-Interval:* Unpredictable time ke baad reward (jaise surprise pop-up discounts).
        *   *Fixed-Ratio:* Fixed number of purchase ke baad reward (jaise buy 5, get 1 free).
        *   *Variable-Ratio:* Random purchase par reward (jaise scratch cards ya lottery ticket). Yeh highly addictive hota hai.

#### 3. Memory Structure and Functioning
*   **Memory Ke Teen Storage Systems:**
    1.  *Sensory Memory:* Bohot temporary storage (1-2 seconds) jab koi sensation aati hai (jaise fresh coffee ki smell). Attention dene par yeh STM mein jati hai.
    2.  *Short-Term Memory (STM):* Active working memory jo current info hold karti hai. Limited capacity hoti hai (7±2 chunks) aur duration bohot chhota (30 seconds se kam).
    3.  *Long-Term Memory (LTM):* Permanent storage jahan unlimited info save ho sakti hai. STM se LTM mein transfer karne ke liye *elaborate rehearsal* (repetition aur meaning samajhna) zaroori hai.
*   **STM aur LTM ka relation:** STM mein current active thoughts hote hain. Repeat ads aur associations se info LTM mein chali jati hai. Jab hum buy karne jate hain, toh LTM se brand image retrieve hokar STM mein aati hai aur purchase decide hoti hai.
*   **Marketer Strategy:** Long-term memory ko target karna chahiye taaki brand loyalty bane, par ad banate waqt STM ki limits ka dhyan rakhna chahiye (simple brand names aur catchy slogans use karein jaise "Amul: The Taste of India").

### UNIT 6: ATTITUDE AND ATTITUDE CHANGE

#### 1. Concept of Consumer Attitude
*   **Definition:** Kisi product ya brand ke prati positive ya negative way mein response karne ki ek learned predisposition (pehle se bani soch).
*   **Measurement Example:** Men's skincare products ke prati attitude ko Likert scale (agree/disagree statements, jaise "Grooming professional success ke liye zaroori hai") ya Semantic Differential scale (opposite adjectives, jaise "Greasy vs. Non-greasy") ke through measure kiya ja sakta hai.

#### 2. The Behavioral Intentions (BI) Model (Theory of Reasoned Action)
Fishbein aur Ajzen ke according, actual behavior ko predict karne ke liye consumer ke Behavioral Intention ($BI$) ko samajhna zaroori hai:
$$B \approx BI = W_1(A_B) + W_2(SN)$$
*   **Components:**
    *   $B$: Actual Behavior.
    *   $BI$: Behavioral Intention (behavior ka best predictor).
    *   $A_B$: Kisi specific behavior ko perform karne ka attitude (jaise sports car *buy* karne ka attitude, na ki sirf car pasand aana).
    *   $SN$: Subjective Norm (society aur family ka social pressure).
    *   $W_1, W_2$: Weights jo batate hain ki personal attitude aur social pressure mein se kaun sa factor zyada important hai.

### UNIT 7: PERSONALITY AND SELF-CONCEPT

#### 1. Theories of Personality
*   **Freudian Theory:** Personality teen internal conflicts ka result hai:
    *   *Id:* Immediate pleasure aur gratification chahne wali drives.
    *   *Ego:* Rational aur conscious control center jo Id aur Superego ko balance karta hai.
    *   *Superego:* Internalized moral values aur society ke rules.
*   **Neo-Freudian Theories (CAD Model by Karen Horney):** Social relationships ke basis par:
    *   *Compliant (C):* Move toward others (pyaar/approval chahiye). Famous brands prefer karte hain.
    *   *Aggressive (A):* Move against others (power/success chahiye). Premium/status brands prefer karte hain to show superiority.
    *   *Detached (D):* Move away from others (independence chahiye). Unique brands prefer karte hain.

### UNIT 8: CONSUMER MOTIVATION AND INVOLVEMENT

#### 1. Concept of Motivation
*   **Motivation:** Woh driving force jo consumer ko action lene par majboor karti hai. Yeh tab generate hoti hai jab koi need adhuri reh jaye aur tension create ho.
*   **Motives:** Behaviour ke piche ke reasons. Yeh *Physiological* (hunger, thirst) ya *Psychogenic* (status, achievement) ho sakte hain.

#### 2. Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs
Needs ke five levels: Physiological $\to$ Safety $\to$ Social $\to$ Ego/Esteem $\to$ Self-Actualization.
*   **Limitations:**
    *   *Strict hierarchy is unrealistic:* Niche ki needs adhuri hone par bhi insaan spiritual self-actualization kar sakta hai (jaise fast rakhna).
    *   *Multi-need fulfillment:* Ek hi product multiple needs fulfill kar sakta hai (jaise restaurant mein khana bhookh bhi mitata hai aur social need bhi).
    *   *Culture-Bound:* India jaise collective culture mein family/group social needs individual esteem needs se zyada important hoti hain.

#### 3. Motivational Conflicts
1.  **Approach-Approach Conflict:** Do positive/desirable options ke beech choose karna (jaise do acche vacation destinations). Marketers extra features ya discounts dekar ise solve karte hain.
2.  **Approach-Avoidance Conflict:** Ek decision ke positive aur negative dono effects hain (jaise tasty sweets khani hai par fat hone ka darr hai). Marketers sugar-free/diet products dekar tension dur karte hain.
3.  **Avoidance-Avoidance Conflict:** Do negative options ke beech fasna (jaise purani washing machine ka expensive repair karwayein ya nayi buy karein). Marketers easy EMIs ya exchange offers dekar help karte hain.
